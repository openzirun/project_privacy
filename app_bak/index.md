# Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

**ScoreBook（胜负簿）**

_Last updated: 2026-06_

## English

ScoreBook is an entertainment score-keeping app. Your privacy is simple with us, because we never see your data:

- **No data collection.** All records you create stay on your device and, if iCloud is enabled, sync only to your personal iCloud account via Apple's CloudKit. We operate no servers and cannot access, collect, sell, or share any of your data.
- **No tracking.** The app contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising, and no third-party trackers.
- **Purchases.** The optional lifetime upgrade is processed entirely by Apple. We receive no personal information from the transaction.
- **Face ID.** When you enable App Lock, Face ID authentication happens entirely on your device through Apple's framework. Biometric data never leaves your device and is never accessible to the app.
- **Deleting your data.** Deleting a record removes it from your device and your iCloud database. Deleting the app and its iCloud data removes everything.

## 简体中文

胜负簿是一款娱乐记分应用。我们从设计上就无法接触你的数据：

- **不收集任何数据。** 你创建的所有记录仅保存在你的设备上；开启 iCloud 后，仅通过 Apple CloudKit 同步到你个人的 iCloud 账户。我们没有任何服务器，无法访问、收集、出售或共享你的任何数据。
- **无跟踪。** 应用不包含任何统计分析 SDK、广告或第三方追踪器。
- **内购。** 可选的终身买断完全由 Apple 处理，我们不会从交易中获得任何个人信息。
- **面容 ID。** 开启应用锁后，面容 ID 验证完全通过 Apple 系统框架在设备本地完成，生物特征数据不会离开你的设备，应用也无法读取。
- **删除数据。** 删除记录即从设备和你的 iCloud 数据库中移除；删除应用及其 iCloud 数据即可清除全部内容。

联系方式：replace-with-your-email@example.com
